    Under the Hood

    How QR Scanning Works

    Four stages of analysis decode your QR code and assess any embedded threats entirely within your browser.

    01
    🖼️

    Image Decode

    The QR image is rendered to a canvas and decoded using the jsQR library — no server upload required.

    02
    📦

    Payload Extraction

    The raw QR payload is extracted and classified — URL, plain text, contact card, Wi-Fi config, and more.

    03
    🌐

    URL Threat Check

    If the payload contains a URL, it is sent to the Google Safe Browsing API for real-time threat analysis.

    04
    📊

    Score & Report

    All signals are combined into a security score with a full breakdown and actionable recommendations.
